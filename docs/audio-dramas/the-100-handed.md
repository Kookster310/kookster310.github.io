# The 100 Handed

**Date:** March 6th, 2026

## Overview

Witchcraft, child abduction, and DNA siphoning are just a few of the body horrors that Wexler and V face. Wexler, a cynic veteran who enjoys chain-smoking as much as gunsmoke,  works for the Madhouse, a group of tattooed magicians that put the ‘cult’ in organizational culture. Everything changes when he brings aboard the dogman V. (Well, dog-woman. Lycanthropy doesn’t discriminate).

## Review

Overall a fun audio drama with a unique story. Gives me ARPG/Diablo vibes. Voice acting is average and writing is okay. Worth the listen
