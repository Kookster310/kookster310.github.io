# Edict Zero - FIS

**Date:** March 2nd, 2026

## Overview

EDICT ZERO was its original name, a codename assigned to a destination planet and the mission to reach it. New Earth, like Old Earth, is a planet of mostly water, but with even less land. It is a small world of five islands, each island a state in The Federation : the democratic entity otherwise known as ‘Edict Two’ which governs the bulk of humanity.

Under the broad authority of its Attorney General, the civilian law enforcement utility Edict Three is a cluster of collaborating agencies such as the FIS — an initialism for Federal Investigative Services.

Assistant Director Alan Dockstader authorized a special unit of the task force to pursue unusual leads and the alternate outside-the-box theories those leads may support.

“Edict Zero – FIS” focuses on this small team of special agents and their investigations, which will put them at odds with the highest authority of humankind:

Edict One.

Whose secrets are getting harder to keep.

## Review

This is up there for one of my favorite audio dramas. It's definitely top 5. The story gets pretty complex and I had to go back and listen to an episode or two on my first go-around, but this series is so freaking good. It's right up my alley in terms of sci-fi with a deep, complex story that evolves so much from episode 1.

The production is top notch, with great sound effects and quality. Voice acting is really good as well, with a familiar voice or two. Special Agent Nick Garrett is one of my favorite characters across all audio dramas. I love how he is written and the voice actor,  James Keller, portrays him perfectly. Some of the main characters are voiced by the same VA, and while he does do a great job, it can takes a little getting used to.

I haven't listened to this story in a few years, though I have listened to it a couple times. I think it's about time for another listen.
