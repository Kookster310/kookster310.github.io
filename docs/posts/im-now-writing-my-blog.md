Well, the most stories start, I needed to get my Amazon cart up to the $35 minimum for free shipping. My 3D printer was recently having issues with the thermistor on the hotend, and I needed to buy a replacement, about $15. So I look at what they are peddling and I come across an entry level fountain pen. AFLOAT Metropolitan PILOT Metropolitan. It's a little over $30, so that will surely get me to my $35 free shipping minimum, right? Maz does quality. So I add a $90 something Moleskine notebook along with some ink for the pen. I finally get my free shipping later. Dam you Amazon, you've done it again.

Anyway, I typically between work, watching sports, playing games/tinkering, etc., I spend quite a lot of time in front of a screen. So I try to think of ways to reduce screen time, while working some parts of my brain that wouldn't... I don't typically use. Why not physically writing blog posts?

I updated my blog post agent SKILL.md to have it read pictures I send it via email. It will put a PR in with the transcribed original handwritten post and a note mentioning the transcription. I haven't actually both it completed it at the time of its writing, but if these words end up on my blog, then it means it worked.

With this change in how I write blog posts, I imagine it will also change how the posts are read. When typing on a computer, I can go back and change or erase entire thoughts. The writing becomes a little more permanent. Sure I can cross out a word or sentence (see very first sentence of this post in handwritten photo), but I try to be a little more thoughtful before putting the ink to paper.

*This blog post was handwritten in ink, then transcribed by AI*
